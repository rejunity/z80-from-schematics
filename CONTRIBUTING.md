# Contributing

Bug reports, hardware measurements, emulator integrations, tests, and
improvements to the C model and RTL are welcome. Include a small reproducer
or a reference to the observed chip behavior when reporting a discrepancy.

## License of contributions

By intentionally submitting a contribution for inclusion in this project,
you agree to license your contribution under the license of the component
you are changing, as described in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE):

- Original C model, support software, tests, and documentation: **MIT**.
- Original hardware sources in `rtl/` and `asic/librelane/`: **CERN-OHL-W-2.0**.
- Third-party components: their existing upstream terms.

You retain your copyright. No copyright assignment or additional right to
relicense your work is requested. Preserve existing notices and add your
own copyright notice where appropriate.

Only submit material you are entitled to contribute under those terms.
Identify any copied or adapted third-party material, its upstream URL and
revision, copyright holder, and license. Include required notices and
license texts. Do not assign MIT or CERN-OHL-W-2.0 to upstream material
whose terms do not permit that grant.

For hardware changes, retain the hardware notices and record the date and
a brief description of the changes as required by CERN-OHL-W-2.0. Add the
modified source's Source Location notice when required by that license.

## Sharing improvements

Pull requests are encouraged. MIT does not require publication of changes.
CERN-OHL-W-2.0 requires source availability in the circumstances it defines;
it does not require contributing changes to this particular repository.

If you use this project, a link to
https://github.com/rejunity/z80-from-schematics helps others find it.
This request adds no condition to MIT. For hardware Products, preserve the
notices and follow the Source Location requirement in
[`HARDWARE_NOTICE.md`](HARDWARE_NOTICE.md).
